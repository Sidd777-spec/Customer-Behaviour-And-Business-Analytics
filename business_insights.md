## 1. Overall - Business Performance 


### Key Findings 
The business generated ₹ 165.68 million in gross sales across 55K transactions, while net sales amounted to ₹ 158.15 million after discounts.

### Business Insight
This represents a ₹ 7.53 million reduction from gross to net sales.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Sales Growth


### Key Findings 
Net sales increased from 2019 through 2023, with 2023 recording the highest full-year sales. 

### Business Insight
The lower 2024 sales figure should not be treated as a decline because the available 2024 data only extends through September.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Product Perfromance 


### Key Findings 
Electronics and Clothing dominate transaction activity and net sales.

### Business Insight
Meanwhile Toys & Games, Pet Care, Other, and Books form a lower-volume group with substantially lower transaction activity and sales compared to remaining 
categories.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Customer Segment + Product Combination Performance


### Key Findings 
Among the combined customer segments analyzed, Female customers aged 25–45 in Mumbai using Credit Card generated the highest net sales. Aditionally 
"Electronics" within the 25–45 age group generated the highest net sales at "₹ 19.21 million" . Thus making this age-category combination the strongest 
product/customer-age combination identified in the analysis.

### Business Insight
It indicate that the 25–45 age group is an important customer segment, particularly for Electronics. The concentration of sales among this segment and the 
identified high-performing customer combination could help the business understand where its strongest customer-product relationships currently exist.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Discount Impact 


### Key Findings 
Discounted and Non-Discounted transactions occured nearly at equal volumes, but non-discounted transactions generated higher net sales and average 
transaction 
value(₹3,012.88) comapred to (₹2,737.19) of discounted.

### Business Insight
However, the higher average transaction value of non-discounted purchases indicates that non-discounted transactions contribute more value per transaction 
than 
discounted purchases.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Geographic Discount Variation 


### Key Findings 
Discount usage was relatively consistent across gender, age group, and purchase method, but showed greater geographic variation, ranging from 45.07% in
Srinagar to 51.29% in Hyderabad.

### Business Insight
Discount usage appears relatively stable across customer demographics and purchase methods, while the variation across locations suggests that discount 
adoption may be influenced more by geographic factors.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Purchase Method Behaviour 

### Key Findings 
Credit Card and Debit Card dominate transaction volume and total net sales, while PhonePe UPI (₹2,936.87) and Paytm UPI (₹2,890.70) have slightly higher 
average transaction values. However, the differences in average transaction value across payment methods remain relatively small.

### Business Insight
Credit Card and Debit Card are the primary payment methods by transaction volume and sales, while differences in average transaction value across payment 
methods remain relatively small.

## 8. Data Quality Consideration


### Customer ID Consistency
Repeated customer IDs were associated with inconsistent demographic attributes such as gender, age group, and location.


### Limitation
The source data / available data does not provide enough information to determine whether these represent shared accounts, multiple individuals, or 
source-data 
inconsistencies. Therefore, the records were retained and customer-level findings should be interpreted with caution.
