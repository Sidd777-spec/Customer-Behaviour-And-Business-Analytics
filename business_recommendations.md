# BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

## 1. Discount Targeting Reviewing

### Based on 
Non-Discounted transactions generated a higher average transaction value despite having a similar transaction volume to Discounted Transactions.

### Recommendation
Need to review how discounts are targeted and consider prioritizing dicounts for customer groups, products, or situations where they are more likely to 
encourage for additional purchases.

### Expected Benefit 
This could help the business use discounts more selectively while protecting the transaction value and overall sales.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Product/Category Focus

### Based on
Electronics and Clothing dominate transaction activity and net sales heavily compared to rest of the product categories.

### Recommendation
Business need to identify and review the factors that are leading to lower contribution of net sales and transaction activities from the other Product
categories. Based on these findings the business could evaluate whether targeted marketing or promotional efforts could help increase the demand for selected 
lower-performing categories.

### Expected Benefit 
Will help the business to increase their sales for these lower-volume contributing categories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Customer Segment Targeting 

### Based on
Female customers aged 25–45 in Mumbai using Credit Card generated the highest net sales. Additionally Electrionics generated he highest sales in this segment.

### Recommendation
The business should further analyze the characteristics of this high-performing Customer-Segment, including purchasing patterns, product prefrences, discount 
usage etc. Similar customer-product combinations could then be evaluated to identify additional high-value segments.

### Expected Benefit
It may help the business to target more such high-value customer-product relationships and support more customer strategies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Geographic Discount Review

### Based on
Discount usage varies more by location compared to gender, age group, and purchase method, where it stays relatively consistent.

### Recommendation
Business need to investigate the geographic factors that are influencing the Discount usage variation. Then determine whether differences in customer 
behavior, product mix, or promotional practices contribute to the variation.

### Expected Benefit
This could help the business understand where discounts are being used more heavily and support more informed decisions about location-specific promotional 
strategies/events.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Payment Method Performance

### Based on 
Credit Card and Debit Card dominate transaction volume and total net sales, while PhonePe UPI and Paytm UPI have higher average transaction values.

### Recommendation
The business should investigate the factors associated with the slightly higher average transaction values observed for PhonePe UPI and Paytm UPI, 
particularly differences in product mix, customer segments, and transaction characteristics.

### Expected Benefit
It could help the business understand whether the higher transaction values are associated with specific customer or product patterns and identify 
opportunities to support higher-value transactions across other payment methods.
